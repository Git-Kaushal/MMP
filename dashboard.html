How to use the mind map
  1. Choose a subject tab: Organic, Inorganic (Anions) or Cations.
  2. On the dashboard, use the filter buttons (Ions & Tests, Principles, Quick Reference, Traps) or just pick any card.
  3. Each card opens only that topic as a small map. You never have to read the whole map at once.
  4. Use Previous / Next (or the ← → keys) to move through the topics in order.
  5. Click a circle on a branch to collapse or expand it. "Expand all" opens every branch.
  6. Scroll / pinch to zoom and drag the background to move. "Fit" re-centres the map.
  7. "Full map" opens the complete original map in a new tab.

How to use this navigator

  1. Choose a mind map. Use the tabs at the top (Organic, Anions, Cations).
  2. Pick one topic. Tap a topic in the list on the left (on a phone, press the ☰ button to open it), or tap a card on the start screen. Only that topic is drawn, not the whole long map. Use the ▶ arrow beside a topic to see its sub-topics.
  3. Move around. Drag the map to move it. Scroll or pinch to zoom. The toolbar at the bottom right has zoom buttons and a fit button that centres the map again.
  4. Open or close a branch. Click the small circle at the end of a branch to fold or unfold it.
  5. Change how much you see. Use Main branches, Sub-branches, Details or Everything above the map.
  6. Go to the next topic. Press Prev or Next to move to the neighbouring topic.
  7. Find something quickly. Type a word in the search box (for example limewater or Fe). The list shows only matching topics.
  8. Share a topic. Copy link gives a link that opens exactly the topic you are viewing. Paste it in WhatsApp or email.
  9. See everything. Show whole map displays the complete mind map.

On a phone, turn the screen sideways (landscape) for a wider view of the map.

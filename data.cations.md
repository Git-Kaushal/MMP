# INORGANIC SALT ANALYSIS — CATIONS
## JEE ADVANCED PRACTICAL CHEMISTRY

### 1. QUALITATIVE ANALYSIS STRATEGY
- Objective
  - Identify the cation present in an unknown salt
  - Use systematic group separation
  - Apply confirmatory tests
- Core principles
  - Solubility product $K_{sp}$
  - Common-ion effect
  - Selective precipitation
  - Acid-base equilibrium
  - Complex formation
  - Amphoterism
  - Redox chemistry

### 2. PRELIMINARY EXAMINATION
- Physical appearance
  - Colour
  - State
  - Solubility
- Flame tests
  - $Na^+$ → intense yellow
  - $K^+$ → lilac/violet
  - $Ca^{2+}$ → brick-red
  - $Sr^{2+}$ → crimson
  - $Ba^{2+}$ → apple-green
  - $Cu^{2+}$ → blue-green
- Important
  - Flame colour is preliminary
  - Do not treat it as final identification

### 3. GROUP 0 — AMMONIUM ION
- $NH_4^+$
  - Add NaOH and warm
  - $NH_3$ evolved
  - Pungent smell
  - Moist red litmus → blue
- Confirmatory
  - Nessler's reagent
  - Yellow → brown coloration/precipitate
- Principle
  - $NH_4^+ + OH^- \rightarrow NH_3 + H_2O$

### 4. GROUP I — CHLORIDE GROUP
- Group reagent
  - Dilute HCl
- $Ag^+$
  - $AgCl$ → white precipitate
  - Dissolves in dilute $NH_3$
  - $[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+$ formation
- $Pb^{2+}$
  - $PbCl_2$ → white precipitate
  - More soluble in hot water
  - $KI$ → yellow $PbI_2$
- $Hg_2^{2+}$
  - $Hg_2Cl_2$ → white precipitate
  - $NH_3$ → black mixture
  - Hg + $HgNH_2Cl$

### 5. GROUP II — SULFIDE GROUP
- Group reagent
  - $H_2S$ in acidic medium
- Principle
  - Acidic medium suppresses $S^{2-}$
  - Only very insoluble sulfides precipitate
  - Controlled by $K_{sp}$ and $[H^+]$

#### GROUP II-A — COPPER SUBGROUP
- $Cu^{2+}$
  - $CuS$ → black
  - Excess $NH_3$ → deep blue complex
  - $[Cu(NH_3)_4]^{2+}$
- $Cd^{2+}$
  - $CdS$ → yellow
- $Bi^{3+}$
  - $Bi_2S_3$ → black/brown-black
- $Hg^{2+}$
  - $HgS$ → black

#### GROUP II-B — ARSENIC SUBGROUP
- $As^{3+}$
  - $As_2S_3$ → yellow
- $As^{5+}$
  - $As_2S_5$ → yellow
- $Sb^{3+}$
  - $Sb_2S_3$ → orange
- $Sb^{5+}$
  - $Sb_2S_5$ → orange
- $Sn^{2+}$
  - $SnS$ → brown/black
- $Sn^{4+}$
  - $SnS_2$ → yellow

### 6. GROUP III — HYDROXIDE GROUP
- Group reagent
  - $NH_4Cl + NH_4OH$
- Principle
  - Controlled $OH^-$ concentration
  - Selective precipitation of hydroxides
- $Fe^{3+}$
  - $Fe(OH)_3$ → reddish-brown
  - $KSCN$ → blood-red complex
- $Al^{3+}$
  - $Al(OH)_3$ → white gelatinous
  - Excess NaOH → soluble aluminate species
- $Cr^{3+}$
  - $Cr(OH)_3$ → grey-green
  - Dissolves in excess strong base
  - Hydroxo-complex formation
- Role of $NH_4Cl$
  - Common-ion effect
  - Suppresses excessive ionization of $NH_4OH$
  - Controls $[OH^-]$

### 7. GROUP IV — SULFIDE GROUP IN BASIC MEDIUM
- Group reagent
  - $H_2S/(NH_4)_2S$
  - Basic/ammoniacal medium
- Principle
  - Higher effective $[S^{2-}]$
  - Sulfides of Group IV precipitate

#### $Ni^{2+}$
- $NiS$ → black
- Dimethylglyoxime (DMG)
  - Rose-red $Ni(DMG)_2$

#### $Co^{2+}$
- $CoS$ → black
- Confirm using an appropriate cobalt-specific test
- Complex formation important

#### $Mn^{2+}$
- $MnS$ → flesh-coloured
- Oxidation chemistry can give manganese(VII)/manganese(IV) species depending on conditions

#### $Zn^{2+}$
- $ZnS$ → white
- $Zn(OH)_2$ → white
- Amphoteric
- Dissolves in excess NaOH

### 8. GROUP V — CARBONATE GROUP
- Group reagent
  - $(NH_4)_2CO_3$
  - In presence $NH_4OH/NH_4Cl$
- $Ba^{2+}$
  - $BaCO_3$ → white
  - Flame → apple-green
- $Sr^{2+}$
  - $SrCO_3$ → white
  - Flame → crimson
- $Ca^{2+}$
  - $CaCO_3$ → white
  - Flame → brick-red
- Principle
  - Selective carbonate precipitation
  - Controlled by $K_{sp}$ and ammoniacal medium

### 9. GROUP VI — REMAINING CATIONS
#### $Mg^{2+}$
- $Na_2HPO_4 + NH_4Cl + NH_4OH$
- $MgNH_4PO_4$ → white crystalline precipitate

#### $Na^+$
- Intense yellow flame
- Confirm with appropriate selective test if required

#### $K^+$
- Lilac/violet flame
- Sodium tetraphenylborate
  - White precipitate
- Flame test is preliminary

#### $Li^+$
- Crimson flame
- Include only where the adopted practical scheme requires it

### 10. HIGH-YIELD CONFIRMATORY TESTS
- $Ag^+$
  - $AgCl$ + dilute $NH_3$
  - $[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+$
- $Pb^{2+}$
  - $PbI_2$ → yellow
- $Cu^{2+}$
  - Excess $NH_3$ → deep blue
- $Fe^{3+}$
  - $KSCN$ → blood-red
- $Al^{3+}$
  - White $Al(OH)_3$
  - Amphoteric behaviour
- $Ni^{2+}$
  - DMG → rose-red precipitate
- $Zn^{2+}$
  - Amphoteric $Zn(OH)_2$
- $Mg^{2+}$
  - $MgNH_4PO_4$ → white crystalline precipitate

### 11. GROUP SEPARATION LOGIC
- Unknown salt
  - ↓ Dilute HCl
  - Precipitate → Group I
  - No precipitate
    - ↓ $H_2S$ in acidic medium
    - Precipitate → Group II
    - No precipitate
      - ↓ $NH_4Cl + NH_4OH$
      - Precipitate → Group III
      - No precipitate
        - ↓ $H_2S/(NH_4)_2S$ in basic medium
        - Precipitate → Group IV
        - No precipitate
          - ↓ $(NH_4)_2CO_3$
          - Precipitate → Group V
          - No precipitate → Group VI / special tests

### 12. EQUILIBRIUM CONNECTIONS
- $K_{sp}$
  - Determines precipitation
  - Lower $K_{sp}$ → easier precipitation
- Common-ion effect
  - Reduces solubility
  - Controls selective precipitation
- Acidic $H_2S$
  - High $[H^+]$
  - Low effective $[S^{2-}]$
  - Selective Group II precipitation
- Basic $H_2S$
  - Higher effective $[S^{2-}]$
  - Group IV sulfides precipitate
- $NH_4Cl + NH_4OH$
  - Controls $[OH^-]$
  - Enables selective hydroxide precipitation

### 13. COMPLEX FORMATION
- $AgCl + NH_3$
  - $[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+$
  - Precipitate dissolves
- $Cu^{2+} + NH_3$
  - Deep-blue ammine complex
- Excess OH⁻
  - Amphoteric hydroxides can form soluble hydroxo species
- JEE connection
  - Stability constant
  - Le Chatelier's principle
  - Solubility equilibrium

### 14. AMPHOTERIC HYDROXIDES
- $Al(OH)_3$
  - Insoluble in water
  - Dissolves in strong base
  - Aluminate species
- $Zn(OH)_2$
  - Dissolves in excess NaOH
  - Zincate/hydroxo-zincate species
- $Cr(OH)_3$
  - Dissolves in strong base
  - Hydroxo complexes
- JEE focus
  - Acid-base behaviour
  - Complex formation
  - Equilibrium shifts

### 15. REDOX CONNECTIONS
- Iron
  - $Fe^{2+} \leftrightarrow Fe^{3+}$
- Chromium
  - $Cr^{3+}$ can be oxidized under strongly alkaline oxidative conditions
  - Chromate formation
- Manganese
  - $Mn^{2+}$ oxidation
  - $MnO_2/MnO_4^-$ depending on medium and oxidant
- JEE focus
  - Oxidation state
  - Oxidizing/reducing agents
  - Medium dependence

### 16. IMPORTANT COLOUR CLUES
- $Cu^{2+}$ → blue
- $Fe^{3+}$ → yellow/brown
- $Fe^{2+}$ → pale green
- $Ni^{2+}$ → green
- $Co^{2+}$ → pink in many aqueous conditions
- $Cr^{3+}$ → green/violet depending on species
- $Mn^{2+}$ → pale pink
- Important
  - Colour alone is NOT confirmation

### 17. RAPID CATION IDENTIFICATION
- White Group I precipitate
  - $Ag^+$
  - $Pb^{2+}$
  - $Hg_2^{2+}$
- Black sulfide
  - $Cu^{2+}$
  - $Bi^{3+}$
  - $Hg^{2+}$
  - $Ni^{2+}$
  - $Co^{2+}$
- Yellow sulfide
  - $Cd^{2+}$
  - $As/Sb/Sn$ subgroup ions
- Orange sulfide
  - $Sb$ species
- White hydroxide
  - $Al^{3+}$
  - $Zn^{2+}$
- Reddish-brown hydroxide
  - $Fe^{3+}$
- Grey-green hydroxide
  - $Cr^{3+}$
- White carbonate
  - $Ba^{2+}$
  - $Sr^{2+}$
  - $Ca^{2+}$

### 18. DECISION TREE
- Unknown cation
  - Flame / physical observation
  - ↓
  - Dilute HCl
    - Precipitate → Group I
    - No precipitate → $H_2S$ acidic
  - ↓
  - $H_2S$ acidic
    - Precipitate → Group II
    - No precipitate → $NH_4Cl + NH_4OH$
  - ↓
  - Hydroxide precipitate
    - Yes → Group III
    - No → Basic $H_2S$
  - ↓
  - Sulfide precipitate
    - Yes → Group IV
    - No → $(NH_4)_2CO_3$
  - ↓
  - Carbonate precipitate
    - Yes → Group V
    - No → Group VI / special tests

### 19. HIGH-YIELD COMPARISONS
#### $Ag^+$ vs $Pb^{2+}$
- Both can form white chlorides
- $AgCl$
  - Dissolves in dilute $NH_3$
- $PbCl_2$
  - More soluble in hot water
  - $KI$ → yellow $PbI_2$

#### $Cu^{2+}$ vs $Fe^{3+}$
- $Cu^{2+}$
  - Blue solution
  - Deep-blue ammine complex
- $Fe^{3+}$
  - Yellow/brown solution
  - $KSCN$ → blood-red

#### $Al^{3+}$ vs $Zn^{2+}$
- Both form white hydroxides
- Both are amphoteric
- Use group behaviour and confirmatory tests

#### $Ba^{2+}$ vs $Sr^{2+}$ vs $Ca^{2+}$
- All may give white carbonates
- Distinguish by flame colours
  - Ba → apple-green
  - Sr → crimson
  - Ca → brick-red

#### $Ni^{2+}$ vs $Co^{2+}$
- Both form dark sulfides
- $Ni^{2+}$
  - DMG → rose-red
- $Co^{2+}$
  - Use cobalt-specific confirmation

### 20. JEE ADVANCED TRAPS
- Flame test
  - Preliminary ≠ final confirmation
- Colour
  - Similar ions may produce similar colours
- $K_{sp}$
  - Precipitation depends on ionic product
- Common-ion effect
  - Important in group separation
- $H_2S$ acidity
  - Controls $S^{2-}$ concentration
- $NH_4Cl$
  - Controls $OH^-$ concentration through common-ion effect
- Excess $NH_3$
  - Can dissolve $AgCl$ through complex formation
- Amphoterism
  - $Al(OH)_3$, $Zn(OH)_2$, $Cr(OH)_3$
- Mixed precipitates
  - A precipitate is not automatically proof of one ion
- Oxidation state
  - Same element can show different reactions
- Medium
  - Strongly affects redox chemistry
- Confirmation
  - Always use an appropriate confirmatory test

### 21. CORE JEE CONCEPT MAP
- Selective precipitation

  - pH control
- Dissolution of precipitate
  - Complex formation

  - Amphoterism
- Colour changes
  - Coordination chemistry
  - Oxidation state
  - Electronic structure
- Redox confirmation
  - Oxidation number
  - Electrode potential
  - Reaction medium
- Group analysis
  - Equilibrium + kinetics + inorganic chemistry

### 22. FINAL IDENTIFICATION ALGORITHM
- Observation
  - ↓
- Preliminary test
  - ↓
- Group reagent
  - ↓
- Selective precipitation
  - ↓
- Separate group
  - ↓
- Dissolve / treat precipitate
  - ↓
- Confirmatory reaction
  - ↓
- Eliminate interfering ions
  - ↓
- Final cation identification

### 23. PRACTICAL SAFETY
- Perform wet tests only in a properly equipped laboratory
- Toxic gases
  - $H_2S$
  - $NH_3$
  - $SO_2$
- Toxic/heavy-metal compounds
  - Hg
  - As
  - Pb
  - Cd
- Use fume hood where required
- Wear gloves and eye protection
- Avoid direct smelling of gases
- Dispose of heavy-metal waste properly
- Do not improvise hazardous gas-generation experiments
